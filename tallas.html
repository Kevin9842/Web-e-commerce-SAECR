SIZE & MEASUREMENT GUIDE

Ensure the ideal fit for every SAECR garment.

1. Chest / Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape measure horizontal to the floor.

2. Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, just above your belly button, without pulling the tape too tight.

3. Hips

Standing with feet together, measure around the fullest part of your hips.
