1. Account Registration & User Responsibility

By registering an account on the SAECR online store, you guarantee that the information provided is accurate, truthful, and complete. You are solely responsible for keeping your access credentials safe and confidential.

Security: SAECR will never ask for your passwords or banking details via email or unverified phone calls.

2. Pre-Order Policies, Limited Production & Delivery Timelines

Garments purchased under the Pre-Order category represent exclusive designer editions in limited workshop production:

  • Item Reservation: Your purchase secures your unit prior to open commercial release.
  • Production Dates: Estimated completion timelines and shipping schedules are indicated on each product page (typically between 15 and 30 business days).
  • Real-Time Tracking: You can check the production stage and itinerary anytime from My Orders in your profile.

3. Payment Methods & Transactional Security

We accept credit and debit cards processed through certified payment gateways featuring 256-bit SSL encryption and compliance with international PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.

4. Guarantees, Size Exchanges & Returns (30 Days)

All purchases made on the SAECR platform include a textile satisfaction guarantee:

  • Size Exchange: You have 30 continuous days following receipt to request a size exchange with zero management fee.
  • Defect Returns: If a garment exhibits any manufacturing defect, we arrange an immediate replacement or full refund.

5. Personal Data Protection & Privacy

Your personal data is processed under strict confidentiality policies. We only use your contact details for order processing, invoice issuance, and delivery logistics coordination.
